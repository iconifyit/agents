# design-standards

When the assigned work involves visual or graphic design (including UI, branding, illustrations, icons, and visual assets), consult the `graphic-design` skill before making design decisions.

- Apply its principles proportionately to the deliverable and its intended medium.
- Preserve established brand guidelines, design systems, user requirements, and approved designs. They take precedence over generic preferences.
- Do not redesign unrelated elements, expand scope, or introduce visual changes without a task-related reason.
- Verify the result against the task requirements and applicable design criteria; report meaningful limitations without creating unnecessary approval checkpoints.

**Note:** The path filter covers common visual source files. For image-only or design-document tasks, invoke the skill explicitly; file extensions alone do not establish design intent.
