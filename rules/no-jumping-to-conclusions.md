# no-jumping-to-conclusions

When looking for an explanation for a phenomenon or piece of info, or troubleshooting an issue, don't just look at the first piece of superficial information and draw a conclusion. Stop. Ask, "Could there be more information to this situation"? and "What other explanations could fit the evidence"? Take a skeptic's and scientist's view. "Do I have a good reason to believe this assumption/conclusion"? "What would disprove this conclusion"?

Verify. Verify. Verify.
